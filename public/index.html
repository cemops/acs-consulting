The Problems We Hear Most

Cemetery boards and managers across the region tell us some version of the same story.

Cremation is rising, and those families go elsewhere

More families choose cremation every year, and without places and programs for them, your cemetery never hears from them.

Pre-need sales have gone flat

Leads go unanswered, the sales process depends on one person, and the numbers haven't moved in years.

Records still live on index cards

Answering “who's buried where” takes a trip to the file cabinet, and one retirement or one fire could wipe out a century of history.

You're running low on saleable space

Existing sections are nearly full, and there's no plan yet for what comes next.

The board keeps turning over

New members start from scratch, and long-term decisions keep getting pushed down the road.

No one is lined up to take over

The person who holds it all together is nearing retirement, and there's no plan for after.

We've worked through every one of these at our own cemeteries. That's the experience we bring to yours.

About ACS

The Same Care We Give Our Own

American Cemetery Services began with a single property: Sherwood Memorial Gardens in Wooster, Ohio, founded by our father in 1961. What started as one family's commitment to serving their community has grown into a multi-generational operation spanning locations across Ohio.

Over the decades we've owned and operated 19 cemeteries, and consulted with more than a dozen others. We've seen every kind of challenge—turnarounds, expansions, regulatory hurdles, staffing issues, board dynamics—and we've developed solutions that work because we've tested them ourselves.

Today we operate five cemetery and funeral home combinations across Ohio, along with two pet cemeteries and a crematory. Since the 1980s, we've also been sharing what we've learned with other cemetery organizations.

When cremation rates began climbing, we didn't wait for burial numbers to fall. We developed cremation gardens, like the butterfly garden pictured here, and began marketing directly to families choosing cremation. It's the kind of forward planning we help our clients put in place.

When you partner with ACS, you're not getting theoretical frameworks. You're getting battle-tested strategies from a family that understands the sacred responsibility we all share.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you work with township, municipal, and church cemeteries?

Yes. We work with cemeteries of every kind, including family-owned, association, church, township, and municipal. We're used to working with trustees and volunteer boards as well as professional managers.

What happens at the complimentary assessment?

We review your inventory, pricing, records, and sales history, then give you an honest read on where the opportunities are. Depending on distance, it may be in person or done remotely. There's no obligation.

How is pricing set?

Every engagement is quoted individually, based on what you need and how involved you want us to be. We have options for every budget, including predictable monthly costs.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many organizations begin with a single consulting session or one project, such as updating rules and regulations or planning a cremation garden, and decide from there.

Who owns our records and software?

Your data is always yours. Every system we set up is in your cemetery's name, so you can keep using it whether or not you continue working with us. Like any subscription software, it stays active as long as your subscription is paid.

Where do you work?

Throughout Ohio and western Pennsylvania.
